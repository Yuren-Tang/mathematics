# Working Mathematics

A public, evolving notebook for mathematical work. Material may be incomplete, incorrect, superseded, split, merged or moved; repository presence is not peer review or final correctness.

## Current projects

- [AffineCDC](projects/affine-cdc/README.md) — affine incidence geometry for cubic binary flows, the independently audited ordinary CDC line, source-fidelity-repaired five-support formulations, and the current mixed-assurance five-support frontier. Its former general component-chain theorem is retained as a failed candidate after an independent third-terminal-path counterexample; local channel tables and models remain supported. Start with the [mathematical architecture](projects/affine-cdc/MATHEMATICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md) or [external-review entrypoint](projects/affine-cdc/external-review/ENTRYPOINT.md), not chronological research notes.

## Organization and chronology

- `projects/` — coherent mathematical projects;
- `unfiled/` — useful material awaiting a natural home.

Active exposition is organized by mathematical dependency, assurance and status. Exact snapshots, migration/supersession ledgers and Git history preserve discovery order, failed candidates, corrections and priority records.

Pushes affecting mathematical trees may receive timestamp manifests on the separate `attestations` branch. Timestamp, signature, tag, release or DOI status does not determine theorem truth.

## Mature outcomes

Stable papers or formal releases may replace evolving notes with pointers while history remains recoverable. No general reuse licence has yet been selected.

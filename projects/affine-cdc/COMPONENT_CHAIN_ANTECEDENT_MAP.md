# Component-chain antecedent map

## 1. Purpose and method

The v9 component-chain mechanism did not arise in a vacuum. This map reconstructs its mathematical ancestry from Git history rather than conversation chronology.

For every entry:

- **source** gives an exact file and frozen SHA;
- **original claim** records the theorem as then stated;
- **then-status** records its contemporaneous assurance;
- **disposition** explains why it was retained, corrected, withdrawn or bypassed;
- **v9 relation** identifies genuine logical dependence, analogy or nondependence;
- **duplication** records rename/rediscovery risk;
- **regression risk** identifies withdrawn machinery that must not re-enter unnoticed.

## 3. Conclusion

The v9 mechanism is **not** merely a rename of one earlier theorem. Its ingredients have antecedents in channel projections, marked boundaries, local root moves, strip/seam geometry and target ranks, but its load-bearing combination is specific:

```text
fixed physical channel
+ distinguished terminal paths
+ singleton inactive vertices
+ witnessed non-channel connector edges
+ inherited shortest path
+ one-edge contraction by ordinary root NNIs
+ final terminal-matching change.
```

The map therefore records both continuity and genuine novelty without assigning publication novelty or priority.

# AffineCDC counterexample and supersession ledger

## 1. Rule of use

A negative result in this ledger remains active until an exact later theorem states and proves a narrower replacement that avoids the witness. A later positive candidate does not erase the old counterexample and does not retroactively validate the refuted inference.

Every entry records:

1. the refuted exact statement;
2. the witness or finite countermodel;
3. the exact source/audit SHA;
4. the retained subtheorem;
5. the replacement theorem or current repair;
6. the inference permanently forbidden.

## 2. Fixed-boundary parent reinsertion failure

**Refuted statement.** After one inverse cancellation, a root-valued alternative insertion with the same exterior four-/five-leaf boundary is already a root state on the literal original predecessor topology.

**Witness.** In equality and good-disjoint rows, the legal inverse insertion may occupy a neighbouring five-leaf topology; in the missing-index row it may carry one normalized atom. The state has the same order and exterior boundary but a distinct source topology plus a target field.

**Source.** `FIRST_CANCELLATION_SINGLE_POP_TARGET_TOPOLOGY_SCOPE_CORRECTION_V1.md`, frozen at `research/...@02b37476198e9eaa2b4cd8d2a2edd76782bdcd49`, internal parent `f4b92f6eb11893f2161560ac898222fa38525f67`.

**Retained subtheorem.** The one pop yields a complete predecessor-order root/one-atom state or a named terminal; distinct-intersecting values give the literal insertion.

**Replacement.** Record the required predecessor topology explicitly, normalize at fixed order, then cross the next stored source NNI.

**Forbidden inference.** `same exterior boundary` does not imply `same literal parent topology`, and boundary-equivalent root existence does not discharge a selected parent obligation.

## 17. Assurance rule

A permanent negative boundary may be:

- independently verified (`I-NEGATIVE`);
- an exact authorial scope correction with a reproducible witness (`III-ACTIVE-NEGATIVE`);
- an unfinished theorem that is prohibited as a premise until proved.

The assurance column matters, but no unreviewed positive theorem may silently override any listed prohibition.

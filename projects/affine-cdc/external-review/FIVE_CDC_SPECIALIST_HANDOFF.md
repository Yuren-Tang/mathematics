## 1. Problem and exact equivalent formulations

The target is:

> Every finite bridgeless multigraph has a cycle double cover using at most five indexed even subgraphs.

After the standard cubic reduction, a five-support witness is equivalently:

- five indexed even edge sets, every edge in exactly two;
- an `R_5`-valued flow;
- a `K_5`-triangle edge labelling;
- an anisotropic flow in `O^-(4,2)`;
- the quadratic cycle-word formulation;
- a cycle-continuous map to the `K_5` cographic target.

These are unordered binary formulations. Integral orientation signs require the separate type-`A_4` root-lift problem.

## 7. Comparison with established approaches

- **4-flow/Catlin reduction.** Published routes begin with a global matching/cycle structure and a 4-flow-bearing remainder. The inverse-pop corpus has no theorem extracting this object from its atoms.
- **Prescribed-cycle/strong 5-CDC.** Known formulations fix a global circuit or 2-regular subgraph and give an extension criterion. The internal “prescribed parent” is a labelled local predecessor, not a prescribed final cycle.
- **Oddness and weak oddness.** Existing sufficient conditions start from a globally bounded 2-factor/even-factor witness. No relation currently derives such a witness from single-pop ancestry.
- **Petersen colouring / cycle-continuous maps.** The cographic formulation gives a conceptual endpoint, but no present argument constructs the required global map from the local rewrite dynamics.
- **Ordinary CDC proof.** Paper A/public ordinary CDC compatibility supplies no missing five-support return theorem; the assured ordinary-CDC and open five-CDC lanes are separate.

Representative comparison sources recorded by the accepted reassessment include Hoffmann-Ostenhof on prescribed subgraphs and 4-flows; Liu--Hao--Luo--Zhang on 5-CDC, 4-flows and Catlin reduction; oddness/weak-oddness sufficient-condition literature; and Petersen-colouring/cycle-continuous-map implications.

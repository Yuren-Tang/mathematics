### Why arbitrary all-index migrations cause no gap

The proof need not orient every edge of the complete horizontal graph.  If a
terminal-free SCC existed, it would be closed under the deterministic
fixed-channel strategy chosen at any one of its states.  That strategy uses
only switches of the selected rescue support and the strict ambient/equal-face
macros, and hence strictly decreases its own transported `Xi` until absorption.
This contradicts SCC closure.  An unrelated missing-index migration may be
viewed as leading to another state with a freshly chosen fixed channel; no
claim of cross-migration numerical invariance is used.

## 7. Exact result and boundary

### Closed here

For every complete fixed-order state whose live prescribed parent central value
is a co-root, there is a finite source-faithful same-order history ending in:

- root flow on the literal parent topology;
- cap-compatible route/profile success;
- named cut/bounded terminal.

The proof is ordinary root NNI plus legal closed support switches and exact
consumers.  It uses no cancellation and no lower-order call.

### Not closed here

parent central value is zero and the two crossed topologies are root-valued.
The fixed-channel DDD frame and `Xi` theorem do not apply.  The status of that
row is isolated separately in

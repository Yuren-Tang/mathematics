## 3. Why the publication gate is not met

The central localization formula is genuine and non-tautological at the local level: `ρ_W` is intrinsic, locally computable and records a hidden plane in `Ann(W)`; in rank four it has a coordinate-free quotient and Pfaffian equation.

Nevertheless, the present global criterion remains equivalent to testing the same affine obstruction against the full equilibrium-stress space. The packet presently supplies no:

- simpler compatibility decision procedure;
- classification of compatible flows or graphs;
- infinite family theorem;
- structural iff criterion avoiding the full stress-space test;
- independently useful downstream theorem beyond the same compatibility class.

The triangular-prism/`K_{3,3}` dichotomy is a useful exact minimal separation example, but not yet an independently citable theory with sufficient external payoff.

1. a family theorem proving compatibility or incompatibility on a natural infinite class;

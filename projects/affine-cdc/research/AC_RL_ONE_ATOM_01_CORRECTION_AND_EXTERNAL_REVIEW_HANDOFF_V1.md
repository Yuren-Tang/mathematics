## 2. Exact defect in the proposed prefix rank

The proposed rank was

```text
(L,mu)
```

where `L` counts remaining literal source NNIs and `mu` is a bounded local
five-leaf atom-relocation distance.

The shared-vertex five-leaf analysis is finite and useful.  The failure is the
disjoint case.  Let `A` be the existing standard atom and `f` the next literal
source NNI with disjoint vertex supports.  Disjointness gives the uncoloured
identity

```text
A f = f A'.
```

It does not imply that `f` is root-valued for the returned labels on its literal
predecessor side.  If that predecessor value is zero or co-root, crossing `f`
creates a second defect disjoint from `A`.  Then:

- the one-atom state type is left;
- no three-vertex bad-overlap normalization applies;
- `L` cannot legitimately decrease;
- `mu` is not defined for the two-component defect locus;
- the v7.2 one-token run/seam theorem is not applicable.

The Heawood certificate realizes exactly this branch with two disjoint `Q_4`
atoms and no route/cut/bounded category exit.

## 5. Why the attempted routes stop here

### Prefix-directed one-atom rank

Fails because a disjoint non-root predecessor creates two persistent atoms
before `L` decreases.

### Five-leaf `C5/C6` rank

Controls only one three-vertex overlap.  The two disjoint atoms do not lie in one
five-leaf carrier, so no bounded `mu<=3` table relates them.

### Local critical-overlap theorem

Its disjoint case assumes the second operation is a legal root Pachner move.
The counterexample's attempted literal predecessor is co-root-valued for the
returned labels, so the hypothesis fails.

### v7.2 run/seam theorem

Requires one supplied nonbranching atom track.  Two disjoint atoms give two
singular components and fall outside its quantified domain.

### Local circuit exclusion

`C5` odd-parity and `C6` identity-lap results exclude local return circuits for
one token.  They do not prevent creation of a second token on a disjoint source
cell.

No component-chain, SCC, generic connectivity or supplied-comparison route is
reopened by this diagnosis.

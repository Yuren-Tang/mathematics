# ONE-ATOM complete endpoint totality

## AC-RL-ONE-ATOM-01 / controlling theorem v1

**Theorem name:** `ONE-ATOM-COMPLETE-ENDPOINT-TOTALITY`  
**Scope:** one post-single-pop standard co-root atom and its literal finite
source-prefix context.  
**Controlling local input:** `ONE_ATOM_PREFIX_DIRECTED_LOCAL_MACRO_CERTIFICATE_V1.md`.

---

## 1. Statement

Let

\[
\mathfrak S=
(G,x,\mathfrak a,R^-,R^+,P,B,\kappa,\sigma,\mathcal D,\alpha,W)
\]

be a complete labelled contextual state arising after the single arbitrary-flow
inverse pop, where:

- `a` is absent or is one standard co-root atom;
- `W` is the exact remaining list of original root-NNI prefix obligations;
- `P,B,kappa,sigma,D,alpha` are the literal live parent, cap, route/profile,
  support, stable-dart and ancestry fields;
- no lower-order call is open.

Then there is a finite source-faithful same-order construction ending in exactly
one of:

1. a complete root-valued crossed endpoint carrying the same live fields, so
   that the audited root/co-root/zero parent-value table is well typed; or
2. an exact current-descendant terminal with its full terminal tuple and
   ancestry map.

The construction uses no same-order root-solubility theorem, generic NNI
connectivity, SCC recurrence, track erasure as target progress, unrelated
recolouring, second cancellation, lower-order call or component-chain result.

---

## 2. Scheduler state

The scheduler state is

\[
(L,\mu;\ C),
\]

where:

- `L=|W|` is the number of literal source-prefix obligations not yet crossed;
- `mu` is zero outside an active overlap macro and otherwise is the number of
  atom-edge advances remaining in the fixed labelled `C5/C6` local path;
- `C` is the finite comparison strip already constructed.

The well-founded rank is lexicographic:

\[
\boxed{(L,\mu)\in\mathbb N^2.}
\]

A transient two-co-root overlap is not a scheduler state.  Its immediate
root-lowering normalization belongs to one atomic local advance.

---

## 3. Scheduler invariant

At every recorded state:

1. `C` is a finite labelled source comparison;
2. the crossed part of the original prefix is represented literally on its
   source boundary;
3. the unconsumed word `W` is unchanged and ordered;
4. there is at most one persistent standard co-root atom frontier;
5. every completed local cell has root-valued long-side restrictions;
6. all stable exterior darts and the ancestry map are retained;
7. the cap and literal parent are not changed by a local choice;
8. route/profile is recomputed after every source move;
9. any exact route/category output is consumed immediately;
10. no root detour has been counted as deletion of a source obligation.

The invariant holds for the initial single-pop collar by the exact source movie:
the missing-index normalization leaves one standard atom, five fixed exterior
darts and the complete contextual fields.

## 4. One scheduler epoch

Assume a persistent atom is present.

### 4.1 `L>0`

Let `f` be the head literal source obligation.

Open the unique bounded macro of
`ONE-ATOM-NEXT-SOURCE-STEP-MACRO`.

- A root endpoint ends the construction.
- An exact terminal ends the construction.
- Otherwise the standard atom reaches the other-order side of `f`.

During a one-vertex overlap, each persistent atom-edge advance lowers `mu`.
When the exact exit is reached, delete `f`, so `L` decreases by one.  The next
local rank may then be initialized from the next literal source step.

No re-selection of a source step, local cycle, exit edge or parent topology is
permitted while `L` is fixed.

### 4.2 No persistent atom

If a local macro becomes entirely root-valued, its current crossed restriction
is a complete root endpoint.  Stop.  The theorem does not continue through the
parent-value table.

### 4.3 Exact terminal

If a target topology is invalid, a route changes, or a named bounded/cut
configuration is exposed, return the exact current-descendant terminal.  No
later source step is attempted.

## 5. Termination of comparison construction

At fixed `L`, an active local rank satisfies `0<=mu<=3` and decreases at every
persistent microstep.  Completion of the macro strictly lowers `L`.

Therefore the scheduler cannot:

- remain forever in one overlap;
- walk once around a local `C5` or `C6`;
- reset its local distance while the same source obligation is live;
- migrate to a different source step without consuming the current one.

The comparison construction terminates after finitely many bounded cells.

This is direct induction on the literal finite source word, not a recurrence on
the set of complete states.

## 6. The `L=0` boundary

Suppose every literal source step has been crossed and one atom frontier
remains.

The scheduler began with the standard post-pop atom collar and marked one of
its two crossed root restrictions by the stored dart order.  Every square or
five-leaf macro extends that collar by a complete labelled comparison cell and
transports the mark on a root long side.  Because the atom locus never branches,
the composite comparison has one open singular track.

When `L=0`, its two short ends are:

1. the marked complete crossed root restriction of the original post-pop atom;
2. the crossed root restriction of the standard atom collar transported to the
   exhausted source boundary.

Both are root-valued by the definition of a standard co-root atom.  The second
is not an unrelated recolouring: its internal roots are forced by the exact
transported boundary word and its topology is the literal source-boundary
resolution reached by the scheduler.

Consequently the constructed comparison has:

- one finite nonbranching normalized atom track;
- root-valued long-side restrictions on every run and switch cell;
- two complete root-normalized short boundaries;
- the same labelled external darts, parent, cap, support and ancestry fields.

There is no unresolved source obligation beyond that boundary.  This is
precisely a **supplied finite normalized-open comparison** in the stated domain
of the retained v7.2 run/seam theorem.

Apply that theorem once:

1. replace every constant-pivot interval by its unique root identity strip;
2. insert the exact six-port root seam at every genuine pivot change;
3. use the retained two-seam treatment for every reduced backtrack;
4. glue on the literal complete root short boundaries.

The result is one root-valued rectangle.  A root path in its source
`1`-skeleton joins the source boundary to the marked complete crossed root
restriction at the post-pop end.

Reverse that path if necessary.  It is the required finite source-faithful
same-order history to the complete crossed endpoint.

## 7. Why the retained state-walk theorem is now sufficient

Before this dossier the available implication was conditional on a comparison
already being supplied.  Sections 2--5 construct that comparison from every
post-pop input by a deterministic induction on the literal source word.

The logical composition is:

```text
post-pop complete atom state
    -> prefix-directed finite comparison construction
    -> normalized-open finite one-token comparison
    -> retained v7.2 run/seam compilation
    -> complete crossed root endpoint.
```

The compilation is not the decrease.  The strict decrease has already occurred
in `(L,mu)` while the comparison was being built.

This removes the exact quantifier defect identified by the independent v7.4.1
audit.

## 9. Exact theorem

### Theorem `ONE-ATOM-COMPLETE-ENDPOINT-TOTALITY`

Every complete labelled post-single-pop state with at most one standard co-root
atom and one literal finite root-NNI source prefix admits a finite
source-faithful same-order disposition to:

1. a complete root-valued crossed endpoint retaining the literal
   parent/cap/route/support/dart/prefix fields; or
2. an exact current-descendant terminal.

### Proof

Initialize the comparison invariant at the single-pop collar.  Induct on the
lexicographic rank `(L,mu)`.  For `L>0`, the bounded next-source-step macro gives
root success, an exact terminal, or the same invariant with smaller rank.  Thus
the construction is finite.  If it ends without an atom, return the complete
root endpoint.  If it ends with an atom and `L=0`, Section 6 gives a finite
normalized-open comparison and the retained v7.2 compilation gives a complete
root-valued crossed endpoint.  All terminal branches are exact by the existing
ledger, and every local cell preserves the complete labelled boundary data.
∎

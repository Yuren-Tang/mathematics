## 3. Formula for every distinct root NNI

Let two distinct adjacent triangles be

\[
T=ijx,
\qquad
U=ijy,
\]

with four distinct supports `i,j,x,y`.  The unique root-valued opposite pairing is

\[
ixy+jxy.
\]

Put support coefficients

\[
c_1=2,\qquad c_2=1,\qquad c_3=c_4=c_5=0.
\]

Then

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\zeta(ixy)+\zeta(jxy)
 -\zeta(ijx)-\zeta(ijy)\\
&\qquad=(c_x+c_y)-(c_i+c_j).
\end{aligned}
\]

### Lemma 3.1 — no neutral distinct NNI

The displayed difference is never zero.

### Proof

Among four distinct supports, either:

- both weighted supports `1,2` occur, in which case the two complementary pair sums cannot both equal `3/2`;
- exactly one weighted support occurs, in which case one pair sum is positive and the other is zero;
- neither occurs, which is impossible because only three zero-weight supports exist.

Thus every root-NNI involution has a unique `Z`-decreasing direction. ∎
